How to play

  1. Pick a fighter, then pack 3 foods for your plate.
  2. Pick an arena. Best of 3 rounds, 60 seconds each.
  3. Out of time? More health left wins the round.
  4. Land hits to fill your super meter, then fire your SUPER.

The menu

  • Cranberry: sticky, slows them down
  • Mash Potato: lobbed, splashes on landing
  • Pumpkin Pie: big knockback
  • Drumstick: heavy hit that stuns
  • Hot Gravy: burns over time
  • Corn Cob: 3-way spread shot
  • Stuffing: steals health back for you

The family

  • Pilgrim Pete: balanced all-rounder. Super: CORN CANNON
  • Tom Turkey: light, can double jump. Super: FEATHER FURY
  • Grandma Gert: slowly heals. Super: ROLLING PIN
  • Uncle Buck: throws 25% harder. Super: HAIL MARY
  • Pumpkin Knight: iron-rind block. Super: JACK-O-BLAST
  • Chef Basil: 30% faster reload. Super: FLAMBE

Controllers: press any button once after plugging in so the browser can detect the pad (a browser privacy rule). The first controller is Player 1, the second is Player 2, and the keyboard works alongside them. Works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox.
